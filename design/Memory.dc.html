Cached memory is not wasted memory. Your Mac keeps recently used files in memory that nothing else needs right now. That is why a healthy machine reports very little free memory, and why DiskMate does not offer to empty it. There is no button here that frees RAM, because freeing it would only make your next few minutes slower.
